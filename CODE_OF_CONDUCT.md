# Code of Conduct

We want WillaAI to be a friendly project where anyone can take part, whatever their background, identity or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions. Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews), and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/8monish). Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban someone who keeps doing so.

This code is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
